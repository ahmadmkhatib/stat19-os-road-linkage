Stage 1 matching retained 23,988 unique control OAs (out of 30,967) and reduced the mean |SMD| by 47.5% (from 0.72 to 0.38), and the maximum |SMD| dropped by 46.6% (from 1.71 to 0.91). Pre-matching imbalance was large for several covariates, such as retail business density (SMD = 1.71) and percentage of households with 2+ cars (SMD = 1.53). After matching, balance improved for nearly all covariates, though residual imbalance remains above the 0.25 threshold for 14 variables, including retail business density (SMD = 0.91), percentage of households with 2+ cars (SMD = 0.83), percentage commuting by car or car passenger (SMD = 0.76), percentage of households with no car (SMD = 0.7), percentage walking or cycling to work (SMD = 0.61), percentage of ethnic minority residents (SMD = 0.5).
