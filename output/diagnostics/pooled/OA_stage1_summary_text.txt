Stage 1 matching retained 9,557 unique control OAs (out of 30,967) and reduced the mean |SMD| by 55.6% (from 0.63 to 0.28), and the maximum |SMD| dropped by 58.3% (from 1.71 to 0.71). Pre-matching imbalance was large for several covariates, such as retail business density (SMD = 1.71) and percentage with 2+ cars (SMD = 1.53). After matching, balance improved for nearly all covariates, though residual imbalance remains above the 0.25 threshold for 13 variables, including percentage with 2+ cars (SMD = 0.71), percentage commuting by car (SMD = 0.71), retail business density (SMD = 0.7), percentage walking to work (SMD = 0.53), percentage aged 45-64 (SMD = 0.42), percentage of White residents (SMD = 0.41).
