Stage 1 matching retained 10,674 unique control OAs (out of 46,218) and reduced the mean |SMD| by 60.9% (from 0.72 to 0.28), and the maximum |SMD| dropped by 65.8% (from 2.21 to 0.76). Pre-matching imbalance was large for several covariates, such as retail business density (SMD = 2.21) and percentage with 2+ cars (SMD = 1.93). After matching, balance improved for nearly all covariates, though residual imbalance remains above the 0.25 threshold for 14 variables, including percentage with 2+ cars (SMD = 0.76), percentage commuting by car (SMD = 0.75), retail business density (SMD = 0.75), percentage walking to work (SMD = 0.55), percentage of White residents (SMD = 0.4), percentage aged 45–64 (SMD = 0.4).
