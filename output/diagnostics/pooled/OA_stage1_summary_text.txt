Stage 1 matching retained 9,177 unique control OAs (out of 30,967) and reduced the mean |SMD| by 67.1% (from 0.72 to 0.24), and the maximum |SMD| dropped by 66% (from 1.71 to 0.58). Pre-matching imbalance was large for several covariates, such as retail business density (SMD = 1.71) and percentage of households with 2+ cars (SMD = 1.53). After matching, balance improved for nearly all covariates, though residual imbalance remains above the 0.25 threshold for 10 variables, including retail business density (SMD = 0.58), percentage of households with 2+ cars (SMD = 0.51), percentage commuting by car or car passenger (SMD = 0.47), percentage of households with no car (SMD = 0.44), percentage of ethnic minority residents (SMD = 0.36), percentage walking or cycling to work (SMD = 0.34).
